Opportunity Scanner — heuristic scoring, NO ML yet

{% if dry_run %}Dry-run — mock/sample scan by default.{% else %}Live mode.{% endif %} Ranked profitable opportunities, scored with transparent rules. The auto-collector builds the ML training dataset in the background (see Training data below).

What this is: the scanner monitors watched Viagogo events, scores each one (market price, recommended price, estimated profit, demand, risk, confidence, best timing) with transparent heuristic rules — see README § Opportunity Scanner. It does not predict the future: there is no ML model yet. The ML training data collects itself — the auto-collector scans your watchlist on a schedule and stores real LIVE prices (Training data section below). Sample data: mock scans use labelled sample numbers, not live market data — do not act on them. Every scan result says clearly whether it is LIVE or SAMPLE. Live scans are switched on in Settings on the main page (Scanner → Live scans).
